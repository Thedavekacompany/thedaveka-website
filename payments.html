Payments, Delivery & Support Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version

  • Digital services, delivered instantly. You get access as soon as your payment is confirmed, normally within minutes.
  • Your card details never reach us. Payments are taken on the secure pages of licensed payment processors, with 3-D Secure where your card supports it.
  • We answer within one business day. Email support@thedaveka.com or call 0808 108 8007.

1. What we sell

TheDaveka Technologies sells software subscriptions. Nothing physical is shipped.

  • TheDaveka Sentinel (app.thedaveka.com): a discipline tool for traders. You set your own trading limits and Sentinel holds you to them on your own exchange or broker account. Sentinel never holds, moves or withdraws your money and does not give investment advice. Plans: Starter, Pro and Discipline, billed monthly, from ₦12,000 (or $9) a month.
  • VEKA (veka.thedaveka.com): an AI receptionist that answers a business's customers on WhatsApp and takes bookings. Plans: VEKA Assistant ₦10,000 a month and VEKA Business ₦20,000 a month, after a 30-day free trial. Annual and set-up packages are quoted in writing before payment.

Prices are shown before you pay and on your receipt. Prices in naira include any processing fees we pass on, which are shown at checkout.

2. How we take payment

We use licensed payment processors: Paystack and Flutterwave. Card payments are made on their secure hosted pages, not on our website. Subscriptions renew automatically each month on the date you first paid until you cancel (see our Cancellation & Refund Policy). Business customers may also pay by bank transfer against an invoice from TheDaveka Technologies.

3. Delivery

  • Instant access: your plan is switched on automatically as soon as the payment processor confirms your payment, normally within minutes. You receive a confirmation by email.
  • Set-up services (for example, setting up VEKA for a business) are delivered on the dates agreed in writing before payment.
  • If your plan isn't active within one business day of a successful payment, email us with your payment reference. We will activate it within one business day, or refund the payment in full if we can't.

4. Processing orders promptly

We process every order as soon as payment is confirmed. We may hold or decline an order that our payment processors or our own checks flag as suspicious, to protect cardholders; if that happens, the card is not charged, or the charge is reversed, and we tell you by email within one business day.

5. Keeping your card details and personal data safe

  • We never see, receive or store your full card number, CVV or card PIN. They are entered only on the processor's page, which meets the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS).
  • Card payments use 3-D Secure (a one-time code or bank-app approval) wherever your card supports it.
  • Our systems use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords only in scrambled form, and limit repeated attempts to sign in or pay.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use your card details for anything except the payment you approved. See our Privacy Policy.

6. Customer service

We respond to every enquiry or complaint within one business day (Monday to Friday, excluding Nigerian public holidays).

  • Email: support@thedaveka.com
  • Phone and WhatsApp: 0808 108 8007
  • Address: TheDaveka Technologies, No 12, Memunat Shitta Street, Ologolo, Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria

7. If something looks wrong with a charge

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank: most issues (a double charge, a charge after cancelling) are fixed the same day, and we refund charges made in error as set out in our Cancellation & Refund Policy. If you do not recognise a charge from TheDaveka, contact your bank and us straight away.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Changes do not affect payments already made.
