Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how THEDAVEKA TECHNOLOGIES ("TheDaveka", "we") collects and uses your personal data when you use TheDaveka Sentinel and thedaveka.com, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). TheDaveka is the data controller. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run Sentinel, under terms that require them to protect it:

  • Paystack: Naira payment processing (Nigeria)
  • Flutterwave: international card payments, where available (Nigeria)
  • SnapTrade: brokerage account connections (Canada)
  • Your exchange or broker: to read your account and place trades you have authorised
  • Replit: application hosting and database (United States)
  • Resend: email delivery (United States)
  • Vercel: website hosting (United States)
  • Meta: advertising measurement

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a regulator or court.

4. International transfers

Some providers store data outside Nigeria, including in the United States and Canada. We transfer data abroad only where the NDPA allows it, with appropriate safeguards in place.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and trading data while your account is active and for up to six years after it closes to meet legal and tax obligations, then delete or anonymise it. API keys are deleted as soon as you disconnect an account or close your account.

6. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption of API keys at rest, hashed passwords, passkey support and restricted access. Sentinel refuses API keys that can withdraw funds, so even if a key were exposed, it could not be used to move your money out.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, receive a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Email support@thedaveka.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Children

Our services are not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
