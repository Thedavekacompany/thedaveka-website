Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how THEDAVEKA TECHNOLOGIES ("TheDaveka", "we") collects and uses your personal data when you use TheDaveka Sentinel, VEKA and thedaveka.com, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). TheDaveka is the data controller, except for the messages that a business’s customers send it through VEKA, explained in the VEKA section below. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run Sentinel, under terms that require them to protect it:

  • Paystack: Naira payment processing (Nigeria)
  • Flutterwave: international card payments, where available (Nigeria)
  • SnapTrade: brokerage account connections (Canada)
  • Your exchange or broker: to read your account and place trades you have authorised
  • Replit: application hosting and database (United States)
  • Resend: email delivery (United States)
  • Vercel: website hosting (United States)
  • Meta: advertising measurement, and the WhatsApp Business Platform that carries VEKA messages
  • AI model providers (Anthropic or OpenAI, United States): to write VEKA’s replies; they process message text under contract and may not use it to train their models

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a regulator or court.

VEKA and WhatsApp messages

VEKA is an AI receptionist that answers a business’s customers on that business’s WhatsApp number. Two kinds of people are involved:

  • Business owners and their staff (our customers): we collect their account details (name, email, password stored only as a one-way hash), business details they enter (name, address, services, prices, opening hours, notes for VEKA), and the WhatsApp Business Account and phone number they connect through Meta’s official sign-up. The access key Meta gives us for that number is encrypted at rest. We use this data to provide VEKA (performance of a contract).
  • The business’s customers: when someone messages a business that uses VEKA, we receive their WhatsApp profile name, phone number and the messages they send, and we store the conversation and any bookings they make. Here the business is the data controller and TheDaveka processes the data on the business’s behalf, only to answer that customer, manage their bookings and send appointment reminders.

How we protect customer messages: phone numbers, emails and similar details are masked before message text is sent to our AI model provider; VEKA never asks customers for personal details; each business can see only its own customers; and we never use these messages for advertising, never sell them and never message people who have not contacted the business first (apart from reminders for appointments they booked).

Retention and deletion: conversations and bookings are kept while the business’s VEKA account is active, so the business has a record of its customers. A business owner can disconnect WhatsApp at any time in Settings, which stops VEKA receiving messages. When a business closes its account, we delete its conversations and bookings within 30 days, except records we must keep by law. A customer who wants their messages deleted can ask the business, or email support@thedaveka.com and we will act on the business’s behalf.

4. International transfers

Some providers store data outside Nigeria, including in the United States and Canada. We transfer data abroad only where the NDPA allows it, with appropriate safeguards in place.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and trading data while your account is active and for up to six years after it closes to meet legal and tax obligations, then delete or anonymise it. API keys are deleted as soon as you disconnect an account or close your account.

6. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption of API keys at rest, hashed passwords, passkey support and restricted access. Sentinel refuses API keys that can withdraw funds, so even if a key were exposed, it could not be used to move your money out.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, receive a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Email support@thedaveka.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Children

Our services are not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
