Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

  • Cancel anytime. No contracts, no cancellation fees.
  • No refunds. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you have paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • Try before you pay. Where offered, the 30-day Founding Member trial lets you use Sentinel without a card.

How to cancel

  1. In the Sentinel app, open Plans and choose "Update card or cancel subscription", then confirm the cancellation; or
  2. Email support@thedaveka.com from the email address on your account and ask us to cancel. We will confirm by email, normally within one working day.

Cancellation stops future renewals. Your plan stays active until the end of your current billing period, after which paid features stop. Your account and settings stay in place, so you can subscribe again at any time.

Refunds

Subscription payments are non-refundable, including for partly used months and for months in which you did not use Sentinel. We make two exceptions:

  • where Nigerian consumer protection law requires a refund; and
  • where you were charged in error (for example, charged twice, or charged after a confirmed cancellation). Email us within 30 days of the charge and we will refund the incorrect amount to the original payment method.

Free trials

Free trials do not require a card and are never charged. When a trial ends, paid features simply stop unless you choose a paid plan.

Closing your account

To close your account and delete your data, use "Delete my account" at the bottom of your Sentinel dashboard, or email support@thedaveka.com. Closing your account cancels any active subscription; the no-refund rule above still applies.
