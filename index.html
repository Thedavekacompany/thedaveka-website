Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about TheDaveka and our products. Can't find yours? Email support@thedaveka.com.

What exactly is the Sentinel?

A discipline tool for traders: think of it as an emotional circuit-breaker. You set your own limits while you're calm (how much per trade, how much per month, which assets you're allowed to buy), and Sentinel holds you to them on the days fear, greed or revenge-trading take over. It connects to your own exchange or broker account and checks trades against your limits.

Does Sentinel hold my money?

No. Never. Your money stays in your own exchange or broker account the whole time. Sentinel is software that connects to that account; it is not a wallet, bank, broker or fund, and we never receive, hold or manage your funds.

Can Sentinel withdraw or move my money out?

No. Sentinel refuses any API key that has withdrawal permission. Where an exchange can't tell us a key's permissions, you must confirm the key can't withdraw before Sentinel will accept it. For MetaTrader, Sentinel only uses your read-only investor password. Even in the worst case, a Sentinel connection can't send your money anywhere.

Does Sentinel place trades for me?

Only if you allow it, and only inside your limits. You choose the access level when you connect:

  • Read-only: Sentinel watches your account and alerts you. It cannot place trades.
  • Trading access: Sentinel can place trades you approve with one tap. If you also switch on Autopilot (off by default), it may place at most one trade a day on its own, still inside every limit you've set.

Any trade above your confirmation amount always waits for your fingerprint or passkey.

Can Sentinel stop me from trading in my broker's own app?

No, and we want to be upfront about that. Sentinel can't block a trade you place directly in your exchange or broker's own app or website. What it does is check your account for those trades and email you if one breaks your limits, so you see it straight away instead of finding out at the end of the month.

Is Sentinel investment advice? Will it make me money?

No and no. Sentinel's recommendations are generated automatically from market data and your own rules. They're not personal investment advice, and Sentinel doesn't predict markets or guarantee profits. Its job is to protect you from your worst decisions, not to promise returns. Trading is risky and you can lose money. See our Risk Disclosure.

Which exchanges and brokers does it work with?

Through SnapTrade: Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, Alpaca, Robinhood, Webull and many more. By API key: KuCoin, Luno and Quidax. Monitoring only: MetaTrader 4/5 brokers such as Exness, HFM and FXTM, and Deriv. The full, current list is on the connect screen in the app.

What happens if Sentinel goes offline?

Nothing happens to your money. Your exchange or broker account keeps working normally; Sentinel just can't monitor or alert until it's back. It doesn't sit between you and your account.

How is my data protected?

API keys are encrypted before they're stored, passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, all traffic uses HTTPS, and you can protect your account with a fingerprint or passkey. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy.

Who is Sentinel for?

Retail traders and investors in crypto, stocks and forex who know their own rules but sometimes break them in the moment, in Nigeria and worldwide.

The Sentinel

A discipline tool for traders, built to catch the moves your emotions would make for you.

What it is

Sentinel is an emotional circuit-breaker. You set your own limits while you're calm: how much per trade, how much per month, which assets you're allowed to buy. Sentinel connects to your own exchange or broker account and holds you to those limits, combining hard rules with AI judgment.

What it does

  • Reads your balances, holdings and trades, with your permission
  • With read-only access, monitors your account and alerts you
  • With trading access, places trades you approve, or Autopilot trades if you switch it on (at most one a day), only inside your limits
  • Emails you when a trade made outside Sentinel breaks your limits

What it never does

  • Never holds your money. Your funds stay with your exchange or broker
  • Never withdraws or moves money out. It refuses API keys that can withdraw
  • Doesn't give investment advice or promise profits
  • Can't stop a trade you place directly in your broker's own app (it alerts you instead)

How to use it

  1. Connect your brokerage — one-time link to your existing account.
  2. Set your guardrails — a per-transaction limit, a monthly allowance, and whether new asset classes are locked.
  3. Let it watch — Sentinel monitors continuously, no manual checking required.
  4. Review the log — every approved or blocked action comes with a plain-language reason.

Benefits

  • Removes emotional decision-making from high-stakes moments
  • Full transparency — every action logged with its reasoning
  • Works alongside you, not instead of you — you set the limits, it enforces them
  • Scales from a single guardrail to full portfolio-wide discipline

Qualities

  • Hybrid intelligence — hard rules and AI judgment together, not one or the other
  • Built for real accounts, with a live brokerage connection
  • Always-on monitoring, nothing to remember to check
  • Designed in Lagos for traders and operators who don't have a safety net
