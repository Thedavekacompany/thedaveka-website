Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Please read this before using TheDaveka Sentinel. Sentinel is a tool to help you keep discipline. It cannot remove the risks of trading.

Sentinel in plain words

Sentinel is a discipline tool: an emotional circuit-breaker for your trading. You set your own limits when you are calm, and Sentinel holds you to them when emotions run high.

What Sentinel does

  • Connects to your own exchange or broker account, with your permission
  • Reads your balances, holdings and trade history
  • Checks trades against the limits you set
  • If you give it trading access: places trades you approve, or Autopilot trades if you switch it on, only inside your limits
  • Emails you when a trade made outside Sentinel breaks your limits

What Sentinel never does

  • Never holds your money. Your funds stay with your exchange or broker at all times
  • Never withdraws or moves money out. It refuses API keys that can withdraw
  • Does not give investment advice or promise profits
  • Cannot stop a trade you place directly in your broker's own app (it alerts you instead)
  • Is not a broker, exchange, bank, wallet or investment fund

1. You can lose money

Trading stocks, cryptocurrencies, forex and other assets is risky. Prices can fall sharply and quickly, and you can lose some or all of the money you trade. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

2. Sentinel does not guarantee profits

Sentinel helps you stick to limits you set. It does not predict markets, and no recommendation it makes is a promise of profit. Past results, including any examples in the app or our marketing, do not indicate future results.

3. Not investment advice

Recommendations are generated automatically from market data and the rules you set. They are not personal investment advice and do not take into account your full financial situation. Consider speaking to a licensed financial adviser before making investment decisions.

4. Automated trading risks

If you give Sentinel trading access and switch on Autopilot, trades are placed within your limits but without you approving each one at the time. Trades may execute at a different price than expected (slippage), may fail or be delayed because of internet problems, exchange outages, API errors or connection issues, and markets may move while a trade is pending.

5. Your limits are your responsibility

Sentinel enforces the limits you choose; it does not decide whether those limits are right for you. Limits set too high still allow large losses.

6. Trades outside Sentinel

Sentinel cannot stop trades you place directly in your exchange or broker's own app or website. It can only detect them afterwards and alert you.

7. Cryptocurrency risks

Cryptocurrencies are highly volatile, are not legal tender in Nigeria, and are not protected by deposit insurance. Exchanges can be hacked, suspend withdrawals, or fail. Regulation of digital assets in Nigeria is still developing and may change in ways that affect your ability to trade.

8. Third-party risk

Your money is held by your exchange or broker, not by TheDaveka. Their security, solvency, fees and rules are outside our control.

9. Currency risk

Converting between Naira, US dollars and stablecoins exposes you to exchange-rate changes and conversion fees.

10. Tax

You are responsible for any tax due on your trading gains.

By using Sentinel, you confirm that you understand these risks and accept full responsibility for your trading decisions and their outcomes.
