TheDaveka Quotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 (Premium verification added)

This policy explains how the TheDaveka Quotes app for Android (the “App”), made by THEDAVEKA TECHNOLOGIES (“TheDaveka”, “we”), handles your information, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). In short: the App does not collect personal data, and we never store, sell or share any. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a Google Play purchase code, and only if you buy Premium (section 2). Our general Privacy Policy covers our other products.

1. What the App stores, and where

InformationWhere it stays
Your settings: chosen quote categories, timing, screen positions, Live mode and battery settingsOnly on your phone, in the App’s private storage
The quote library and background scenesBuilt into the App; no internet connection is needed to show quotes
Quote images you choose to shareCreated on your phone and passed only to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp); a temporary copy stays in the App’s private cache

The App has no account or sign-in, does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or messages, and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

2. Premium subscriptions

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your name, email, card or payment details. You can cancel any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

How we confirm Premium. As Google recommends, when you buy Premium (and from time to time afterwards) the App sends the purchase code Google Play gives it, called a purchase token, to our server at thedaveka.com over an encrypted connection. Our server asks Google Play whether that subscription is genuine and active and tells the App the answer. The purchase token does not identify you by name. We use it only for this check and to prevent fraud, and we do not store or log it. If our server cannot be reached, the App relies on Google Play on your phone until it can.

3. Sharing with others

We do not sell any information from the App. The purchase token described in section 2 is sent only to Google Play, to confirm your subscription.

4. Deleting your data

Everything the App stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, deletes it completely.

5. Security

Settings are kept in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Shared images are created only when you tap Share.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Your rights and contact

Under the NDPA you may ask us about any personal data we hold about you; for the App, we hold none. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply. Questions: support@thedaveka.com, TheDaveka Technologies, 18, Ologolo via Baruwa Road, Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Changes

If the App changes in a way that affects your data (for example, a feature that needs an internet connection), we will update this page and the App’s store listing before the change goes live.
