How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open (or find it in Files › Downloads).
  3. If your phone says installing from your browser isn’t allowed, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may say the developer is unknown, because the app isn’t on the Play Store yet: tap More details › Install anyway.
  5. Open TheDaveka Quotes and tap Set as my wallpaper.

Updating? Download again and install over the old version. If your phone says “App not installed”, uninstall the old copy first. This is needed once if you installed a copy before 15 November 2026; later versions update in place.
