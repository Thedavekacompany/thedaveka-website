Sentinel in plain words

Sentinel is a discipline tool: an emotional circuit-breaker for your trading. You set your own limits when you are calm, and Sentinel holds you to them when emotions run high.

What Sentinel does

  • Connects to your own exchange or broker account, with your permission
  • Reads your balances, holdings and trade history
  • Checks trades against the limits you set
  • If you give it trading access: places trades you approve, or Autopilot trades if you switch it on, only inside your limits
  • Emails you when a trade made outside Sentinel breaks your limits

What Sentinel never does

  • Never holds your money. Your funds stay with your exchange or broker at all times
  • Never withdraws or moves money out. It refuses API keys that can withdraw
  • Does not give investment advice or promise profits
  • Cannot stop a trade you place directly in your broker's own app (it alerts you instead)
  • Is not a broker, exchange, bank, wallet or investment fund

1. What Sentinel does

1.1 Sentinel is risk-management software. It lets you set your own trading limits, such as the most you can spend per trade, the most you can spend per month, which asset classes you may buy, and an amount above which you must confirm with your fingerprint or passkey, and it helps you stay within them.

1.2 With your permission, Sentinel connects to your own account at a supported exchange or broker (a "Connected Account") through an API key, a read-only login, or a connection service such as SnapTrade. Depending on the access you choose, Sentinel can:

  • Read-only access: read your balances, holdings and trades, check them against your limits, and alert you. It cannot place trades.
  • Trading access: also place trades in your Connected Account, but only trades that pass every limit you have set: trades you approve in the app, and, if you switch on Autopilot, trades Sentinel proposes on its own (at most one Autopilot trade per day). Trades above your confirmation amount always wait for you.

1.3 Where a proposed trade would exceed your limits, Sentinel may place only the part that fits within them, or not place it at all. On the Discipline plan, Sentinel can complete a larger trade in smaller steps over time, re-checking each step against your limits and live portfolio before placing it.

1.4 Sentinel never holds your money or assets. Your funds stay in your Connected Account at all times. Sentinel does not ask for, and refuses, any permission to withdraw or transfer funds out of your Connected Account.

1.5 Sentinel cannot block trades made outside it. If you place a trade directly in your exchange or broker's own app or website, Sentinel cannot stop it. It checks your account for such trades and emails you if one breaks your limits.

1.6 Sentinel is not a broker, exchange, bank, custodian, wallet, fund manager or investment adviser. Recommendations are generated automatically from market data and the rules you set. Nothing in the Service is investment, financial, legal or tax advice.
